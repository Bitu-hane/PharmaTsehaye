Our story

A neighborhood pharmacy that takes time to explain.

Tsheaye Unique Pharmacy was established on Bole Road with a single, clear objective: to bring warmth, unhurried human listening, and rigorous clinical precision to everyday community healthcare.

In an environment where healthcare visits can often feel rushed and transactional, we believe the counter should be a sanctuary of clarity. Our licensed clinical pharmacists take the necessary time to review dosages, evaluate multi-drug interactions, and answer your family's health questions without watching the clock.

From newborn pediatric care to elderly chronic condition management, we combine verified international pharmaceutical formulations with trusted botanical wellness options, ensuring every patient leaves empowered and confident.

What we stand for

Integrity First

Genuine only

Counterfeit and degraded pharmaceuticals are a serious global concern. At Tsehay, we enforce a strict zero-compromise procurement policy. Every blister pack, syrup, and injection is sourced through certified supply chains with verifiable batch numbers.

Patient Empowerment

Clear explanations

Healthcare should never feel intimidating. We believe you have the right to understand exactly what goes into your body. We take the time to explain active ingredients, anticipated side-effects, food interactions, and correct timing in plain, accessible language.

Community Dedication

Care for everyone

From first-time walk-in visitors to families we have served across generations, we treat every patient like our own. Our on-call pharmacist is available even during public holidays so you never face a health crisis alone.

Clinical Excellence

Our 3 Core Safety Guarantees

How we protect your family's health every single day.

Standard 01

Continuous Cold-Chain Control

Insulins, vaccines, and biologic eye drops are preserved under strict temperature-controlled commercial refrigeration (2°C–8°C) with continuous digital monitoring to guarantee potency.

Standard 02

Double-Check Verification

Every prescription is verified against clinical databases for dosage accuracy, patient age appropriateness, and potential cross-reactions with current prescriptions before dispensing.

Standard 03

Direct Pharmacist Access

You never have to speak through an intermediary. When you call or visit, you consult directly with a qualified, licensed healthcare professional who listens and guides you.
