Prescription Service

How to Confirm & Reserve Your Medicines

Save time and ensure your items are ready before you arrive at Bole Road.

Step 01

Send Your Prescription

Take a clear photo of your doctor's prescription note or write down the medicine name and dosage. Send it directly to our pharmacist via WhatsApp (+251 91 108 6624).

Step 02

Pharmacist Review & Reservation

Our licensed clinical team verifies availability, checks for alternative formulations if needed, confirms pricing, and reserves your items in our holding area.

Step 03

Pickup & Consultation

Visit our pharmacy on Bole Road for fast collection. We review administration timing, explain precautions, and answer all your questions in person.

Formulary Standards

Our 3-Point Formulary Quality Commitment

Why families and healthcare providers trust Tsehay Unique Pharmacy.

Standard 01

100% Verified Sourcing

Every box and blister pack on our shelves is obtained directly through certified distributors with full batch tracking and tamper-evident authentication.

Standard 02

Active Cold-Chain Storage

Temperature-sensitive medications like insulins, eye drops, and vaccines are continuously maintained in clinical-grade refrigeration units with digital logging.

Standard 03

Clinical Interaction Check

Before handing over any medication, our pharmacists cross-reference your new prescription with any ongoing treatments to prevent adverse drug interactions.
