Medicine Use Guidance

Master device technique and safe administration.

A medication can only deliver its intended benefit when administered correctly. From specialized asthma inhalers to insulin delivery pens and pediatric oral syringes, technique directly determines therapeutic success.

Many treatment difficulties arise simply from an incorrectly primed inhaler or poor inhalation speed. Our pharmacists offer patient, hands-on demonstrations in our consultation area until you feel completely confident managing your routine at home.
